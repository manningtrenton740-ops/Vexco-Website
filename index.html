About Vexco LLC

Vexco LLC is an independent, solo-operated roadside assistance provider based right here in Hot Springs, Arkansas. When you request service, you are dealing directly with the operator handling your call and your vehicle.

We focus strictly on core mechanical roadside services to get you back on the road safely without the runaround of a massive corporate dispatch network.

Our Services & Pricing

⚡ Jump Starts --

Dead battery? We provide safe, reliable heavy-duty jump-starts to get your engine turning over.

🛞 Flat Tire Changes --

Got a flat? We will safely swap out your disabled tire using your spare tire.

🔓 Vehicle Lockouts --

Keys locked inside your vehicle? Professional, damage-free entry tools used to get you back inside.

Service Scope Notice

To guarantee steady operations for our core offerings, Vexco LLC exclusively handles jump-starts, flat tire changes using spares, and vehicle lockouts. Please note that we do not offer towing services or fuel delivery.
